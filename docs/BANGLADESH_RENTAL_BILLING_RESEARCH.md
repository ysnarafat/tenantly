# Bangladesh Apartment Bills: Research Summary

**Researched:** November 9, 2026

## Summary

Tenants in Bangladesh may pay utilities separately, while building-wide costs are bundled into a service charge or itemized as separate fees. The mix depends on the building and rental arrangement. The sources reviewed do not establish one nationwide standard or formula for dividing shared costs among flats.

## Common charge types

| Type | Examples found in the research | Product considerations |
|---|---|---|
| **Unit utilities** | Electricity, water, piped gas or LPG. Some listings bundle gas or water into the service charge; others say utilities are paid separately. | Record whether each bill is for an individual meter/account or shared by the building. Distinguish piped, prepaid, and LPG-cylinder arrangements for gas. |
| **Shared building costs** | Security, lift operation, common-area electricity, caretaker and cleaning. A Dhaka public-housing case reported a fixed service charge for security, elevators and common electricity, in addition to individual gas, water and electricity bills. | Show what a service charge includes instead of treating it as an unexplained amount. |
| **Other separate or variable charges** | Generator costs, waste collection, parking and Internet appear in some rental examples. | Make these optional, separately named charges; they may be included, separately billed, or tenant-arranged depending on the property. |
| **One-off expenses** | Repairs or maintenance can arise, but should not automatically be treated as a tenant bill. | Record separately and identify the contractual or other basis for charging it. |

These are examples, not prevalence estimates. The [bdnews24 report on a public-housing project](https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/018q7x9k3u) describes separate utility bills plus a fixed shared-services charge. Rental advertisements also show different bundles, including examples in [Uttara](https://www.bdhousing.com/details/249682/family-apartment-flats-rent-uttara) and [Mirpur](https://www.bdhousing.com/details/245560/513-a-1000-sqft-flats-for-rent-at-mirpur-13). Advertisements are useful examples of practice, but are not a representative survey.

## Utility tariffs and legal context

Utility bills should not be calculated using one assumed rate. [DPDC's tariff schedule](https://dpdc.gov.bd/site/page/27e77af7-1187-436c-9f80-b579d3493a46/Tariff-Rate) distinguishes customer classes and billing components; its schedule effective June 2026 includes residential consumption slabs and demand charges. [Dhaka WASA's tariff page](https://dwasa.org.bd/site/page/224276b5-882c-4f19-b761-ee1bcbd3cee4/Water-Tariff) publishes dated price schedules, including one effective July 1, 2024. For gas, [Titas publishes tariff orders](https://titasgas.gov.bd/site/page/c3f5af23-ca40-44cc-aa3f-31b6546c7e56/), while [BERC publishes LPG price notices](https://berc.org.bd/site/page/e354f536-6775-4523-8575-2cf671e98c6f/).

The [Premises Rent Control Act, 1991](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-details-748.html) contains provisions on rent, receipts, municipal charges and maintenance of essential services. The Act reviewed did not provide a general formula for allocating shared building expenses such as generator fuel, lift costs or security among flats. Allocation should therefore be recorded as an arrangement specific to the property or rental agreement, not presented as a legally prescribed default. This is product research, not legal advice; consult Bangladesh counsel about a specific arrangement or dispute.

## Recommendations for Tenantly

1. Keep **rent**, **unit-level utilities**, and **shared building expenses** distinct.
2. For each bill, record its period, amount, source or bill image, and whether it is fixed, meter-based, or shared.
3. Let the manager choose and document the allocation method—for example, actual submeter usage, equal share, area-based share, or a fixed agreement amount—and identify any units excluded.
4. Make the resulting printout itemized and easy to check. Show the charge, period, amount, and allocation basis.
5. Add charge types incrementally: the current manual, building-by-building electricity register can be followed by water, gas, generator, waste, parking and other charges as needed.

## Source notes

- **Official tariff and law sources** establish published tariff schedules and statutory text; they do not, by themselves, determine a particular building's tenant allocation.
- **Rental advertisements** and the **bdnews24 report** provide concrete examples of billing arrangements, but are location-specific and non-representative.
- The research found no robust public survey establishing a nationwide standard for service-charge components or allocation formulas.
